## The core problem with current pricing

The CLAUDE.md states $597–$997/mo retainer. Here is why that is misaligned with the target client:

**Target client profile (from DM qualifier rubric):**
- 1–5 paid gigs/year
- Keynote fee: $2,500–$5,000
- Gross annual speaking revenue: ~$5,000–$25,000

**What $597/mo means to them:**
- $7,164/year = 29–143% of their annual speaking revenue
- For a client earning $5k/year from speaking, Reeve costs more than speaking pays
- For a client at $25k/year, Reeve is 29% of gross — still very high before accounting for taxes and travel

**Conclusion:** The current price structure is correct for clients earning $50k+/year from speaking. It is a dealbreaker for the target segment Reeve is designed to serve. Either the price must come down or the target client must move up.

**Key insight:** No one in this market offers systematic outbound booking at an accessible price point. That gap is Reeve's actual opportunity.

## Three viable pricing models

### Model A: Pure Performance (no retainer)
**Structure:** 15–20% of each confirmed booking fee  
**Example:** Client books a $5,000 gig → Reeve earns $750–$1,000

**Pros:**
- Zero barrier to entry — speakers pay nothing until Reeve delivers
- Aligns incentives perfectly
- Easier to sell ("we only win when you win")
- No churn — no monthly bill to cancel

**Cons:**
- Revenue is unpredictable and lumpy
- Dave bears all upfront cost of pitching
- Clients may accept lower-fee gigs to avoid sharing
- Hard to run at scale without an escrow/tracking system

**Best for:** Early market validation. Prove you can book gigs before charging retainers.

---

### Model B: Tiered Retainer (recommended for current stage)
**Structure:**

| Tier | Price | What's included |
|------|-------|----------------|
| **Scout** | $97/mo | Weekly CFP digest emailed to speaker; opportunity list only; no pitching |
| **Pitch** | $297/mo | Scout + Reeve pitches on their behalf (up to 8/mo); follow-ups; pipeline report |
| **Full** | $597/mo | Pitch + one-sheet writing; demo reel consulting; negotiation; closer emails |

**Why this works:**
- $97 is an impulse buy for a speaker with any income
- $297 is the "serious" tier — speaker is motivated, not just curious
- $597 is correct for clients already earning $30k+/year from speaking
- Natural upgrade path as the speaker grows
- MRR is predictable

**Revenue model at scale:**
- 50 Scout × $97 = $4,850
- 20 Pitch × $297 = $5,940
- 10 Full × $597 = $5,970
- **Total MRR: $16,760 (~$201k ARR)**

**At 100 clients (achievable in 18 months with Milly running):**
- Same ratio → **$33,500 MRR (~$402k ARR)**

---

### Model C: Hybrid Retainer + Performance
**Structure:** Reduced retainer + % of bookings above a threshold

| Tier | Monthly base | Performance fee |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| Starter | $197/mo | 10% of each booking |
| Pro | $397/mo | 8% of each booking |

**Why this can work:**
- Lower base reduces barrier
- Performance fee keeps Reeve motivated
- At $5,000/gig, 10% = $500 → Reeve earns $697 total that month vs $297 flat
- Upside for both sides

**Risk:** Tracking and collecting % fees requires infrastructure (contracts, invoicing). Not recommended until you have 10+ paying clients and a proven booking rate.

## Recommended path

### Phase 1 (now — first 90 days): Validate the value
Run Reeve for **3 clients at no charge** or **performance-only (15%)**. Goal: prove you can get a speaker booked within 90 days. Collect one testimonial per client.

**Why:** $597/mo with zero proof of bookings is a hard sell. One real booking story closes deals faster than any pricing page.

### Phase 2 (90–180 days): Launch tiered retainer
Once you have 1–3 booking success stories, launch publicly:
- Scout: $97/mo
- Pitch: $297/mo
- Full: $597/mo

Milly's CTA shifts from "DM stages" → "DM stages to get your free pipeline audit."

The free audit (15-min call) qualifies them and places them in the right tier. High-fit → Full. Mid-fit → Pitch. Low-fit → Scout or decline.

### Phase 3 (6–12 months): Add performance layer
Once MRR > $10k and booking rate is proven, add 8–12% performance fee on top of retainer for Pro and Full tier. Justify it as "Reeve earns when you earn." Price the base retainer down slightly to absorb the optics.

## What the DM qualifier should actually score for

The current scoring rubric (paid gigs + fee + topic specificity) is screening for Full-tier clients only. It is rejecting people who are perfect for Scout or Pitch tier.

**Recommended qualifier revision:**
- high → Full tier ($597/mo) — 5+ gigs, $5k+ fee, specific niche
- mid → Pitch tier ($297/mo) — 1–4 gigs, $2,500–$5k fee, some specificity
- low → Scout tier ($97/mo) — 0 paid gigs but motivated + clear niche
- decline → Still no paid gigs AND vague niche AND no clear motivation

**Impact:** Conversion rate likely doubles. Low-fit leads become $97/mo recurring revenue instead of warm declines.
