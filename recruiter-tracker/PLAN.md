## Templates (4, each kept under 120 words)

### A — LinkedIn search (cold, no mutual connection)
> Hi [Name] — I noticed you recruit for [industry] commercial and finance leadership
> roles. I'm a Director-level commercial/finance leader — nearly two decades inside one
> organization, moving from national accounts into pricing policy and margin governance
> for a $250M business unit within a $1.3B portfolio. I'm exploring [Role Title]-type
> opportunities, ideally in [industry], Denver-based or open to remote. Would you be
> open to a short call to see if I'm a fit for anything on your desk, now or upcoming?
> Happy to send my resume ahead of time. Thanks for considering — David

### B — Reverse-engineered from a posting
> Hi [Name] — I came across the [Role Title] posting at [Firm] and wanted to reach out

> improvement through category pricing. That sales-to-finance crossover seemed relevant

## Intake call — 30-second frame

> I'm a commercial and finance leader who's spent nearly two decades inside one
> organization — moving from front-line customer service to national account sales to
> owning pricing policy, margin governance, and executive reporting across a $1.3
> billion revenue scope. I built a segment from zero to a $10-12 million peak,
> personally carrying about $6 million of it, then crossed to the other side of the
> table — building my company's first price-to-volume governance loop and driving real
> margin improvement through pricing strategy. Sales leaders with real finance depth are
> rare. Finance leaders who've personally grown accounts are rare. I'm both, and I'm
> looking at Director-level Sales or Finance roles, ideally in [industry], based in
> Denver or open to remote.

## Key metrics to have ready on the call

- $250M business unit — pricing policy, margin governance, rebate + price approval authority
- ~$1.3B combined revenue scope for executive/investor reporting
- ~$1.4M annual sales lift — built the price-to-volume governance loop
- ~$500K annual gross margin improvement — water heater category pricing
- Multifamily segment: built from $0 to $10-12M peak, ~$6M personally carried
- Lowe's account: grew from $4M to $8M (stable $7-10M today)

## Four questions to ask the recruiter

1. What is the client actually trying to solve with this hire — not just the title, but the business problem?
2. What's the realistic compensation range, and is there flexibility based on experience?
3. Who else are you presenting for this role, and how do I compare?
4. What's the timeline, and what's the next step after this call?

## Phase 4 — monthly maintenance checklist

- [ ] Re-engage any "Replied" or "Intake call" contacts gone quiet 3+ weeks
- [ ] Move fully-cold contacts to "Dead" with a closing note
- [ ] Refresh the target recruiter list — new firms/contacts in [industry]
- [ ] Review the four templates against what's actually gotten replies; tighten wording
- [ ] Confirm LinkedIn profile is current
- [ ] Export/back up the CSV
- [ ] Check weekly-cap discipline — did the last 4 weeks actually stay near 5/week?
